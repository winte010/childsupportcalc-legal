How Ohio Calculates Child Support

Ohio's Income Shares Model combines both parents' income with parenting time and healthcare considerations

Combined Gross Income

Total income from both parents: wages, self-employment, bonuses, rental income, investment earnings, and other sources.

Deductions

Subtract federal/state income taxes, FICA, health insurance, mandatory retirement, and support for other children.

Income Cap: $336,000

Ohio's basic schedule applies to combined income up to $336,000 annually. Above this, support is determined case-by-case but cannot be less than guideline amounts.

Basic Schedule Lookup

Use Ohio's guidelines schedule to find base support based on combined income and number of children.

Parenting Time Credit

A 10% deduction applies when a parent has 90+ overnights annually (25% or more parenting time).

Healthcare Allocation

Health insurance costs are allocated proportionally between parents and added to base support.
