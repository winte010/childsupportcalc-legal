How Michigan Calculates Child Support

Michigan's formula-based approach combines multiple components into a comprehensive support calculation

Michigan Formula Components

Net Income

Base monthly net income of paying parent(s)

Healthcare

Health insurance premiums for the child

Childcare

Work-related and education-related childcare expenses

Education

Extraordinary education expenses beyond regular schooling

Parenting Time

Adjustment based on overnight percentages and custody time

Other Factors

Court can consider additional statutory deviation factors

Overnight Calculation

Michigan counts actual overnight stays with the child. Parenting time credit is calculated as a percentage of overnights per year.

Medical Support

Health insurance premiums and uninsured medical expenses are handled separately from base child support calculations.

Net Income Calculation

Gross income minus taxes, FICA, health insurance, union dues, and mandatory retirement contributions.

Multiple Children

Support amounts increase for each additional child based on Michigan's formula adjustments.

Shared Parenting

When both parents share significant parenting time, the formula adjusts to account for both households' needs.

Annual Review

Michigan law allows support orders to be reviewed and potentially modified when circumstances change significantly.
